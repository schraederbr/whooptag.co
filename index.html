What is WhoopTag?

WhoopTag is a small PCB for tiny whoop drones that allows you to play laser tag against other whoops. It adds an IR emitter, IR receivers, and Betaflight OSD integration.

The goal is to make it easy to mount on existing whoop stacks, connect to the flight controller, and play.

Key Features

IR Laser Emitter

Tight IR beam fires on trigger input. Safe, invisible to the naked eye.

360° IR Receivers

Sensors wrap the board - no blind spots from any angle.

Whoop Form Factor

25.5x25.5mm standard mount. Fits your existing stack.

Hit LED Indicator

RGB LED flashes on impact - visible in FPV and line of sight.

Health & Lives System

Configurable HP and lives. Auto-disarms after X hits.

1S LiPo Compatible

Runs off your existing 1S battery. No extra hardware needed.

Team Mode

Assign drones to red or blue teams. Toggle friendly fire on or off per session.

Game Mode Support

Supports Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, and more game formats out of the box.

OSD Integration

Live HP and lives count displayed directly in your FPV goggles via OSD.

Buzzer Output

Audible beep on hit and a distinct tone on elimination. Works with standard buzzers.

FC Integration

Connects via UART to Betaflight. Hit events can trigger motor stops or other FC responses.

Configurable Fire Rate

Adjust shot cooldown and burst mode settings to balance gameplay.
