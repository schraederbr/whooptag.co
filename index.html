Features

OSD Integration

HP and hit count displayed directly in your FPV goggles via Betaflight OSD.

IR Emitter & Receivers

High power IR transmitter and receivers around the board.

Wireless Communication

Sync hits, player stats, and game data across multiple WhoopTag boards.

Whoop Form Factor

26x26mm standard mount. Fits on your existing AIO.

External Scoreboard

Use a web browser to view the game scoreboard live.

5V BEC Powered

Runs off any 5v BEC with at least 500ma output power.

Game Mode Support

Supports lives, deathmatch, and more.
