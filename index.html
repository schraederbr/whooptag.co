A custom PCB that brings laser tag to your whoop fleet.

What is WhoopTag?

WhoopTag is a small PCB for tiny whoop drones that allows you to play laser tag against other whoops. It adds an IR emitter, IR receivers, a wireless MCU and integrates with Betaflight OSD.

It mounts on top of 26x26mm AIOs, and is light enough to work on any whoop.

Features

IR Emitter

Tight IR beam. Invisible to the naked eye.

360° IR Receivers

Sensors all around the board.

Whoop Form Factor

26x26mm standard mount. Fits on your existing AIO.

LED Player Indicator

Players can choose a custom color.

1S+ Compatible

Runs off any 5v BEC with at least 400ma output power.

Game Mode Support

Supports Tag, Deathmatch, and more game formats out of the box.

OSD Integration

HP and hit count displayed directly in your FPV goggles via Betaflight OSD.
